COMMUNITY STANDARD

Celebrate well.
Respect everyone.

Nigerian Reunion is built for culture, connection and celebration. Everyone attending, working or participating is expected to help protect that environment.

Respect

Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, threats, stalking, unwanted sexual attention, targeted abuse or demeaning conduct toward guests, staff, artists, vendors, creators or partners is not acceptable.

Safety

Follow instructions from security, venue personnel, medical staff and event management. Do not enter restricted areas, interfere with emergency operations, create unsafe crowd conditions or bring prohibited items.

Consent

Respect personal boundaries. Do not touch, photograph, film or repeatedly approach another person in a way that is threatening, invasive or clearly unwanted.

Alcohol and substances

Guests are responsible for their behavior regardless of alcohol consumption. Illegal drugs, unlawful possession, unsafe intoxication and supplying alcohol to underage guests are prohibited.

Property

Do not damage venues, furniture, tents, equipment, vehicles, landscaping, vendor property or another guest’s belongings. Individuals may be held responsible for damage they cause.

Weapons and dangerous items

Weapons, explosives and other dangerous items are prohibited except where expressly authorized by law and venue policy. Security screening procedures may vary by venue.

Enforcement

Depending on the situation, violations may result in a warning, removal, revoked credentials or tickets, denial of future access, or referral to venue security or law enforcement. Refunds are not guaranteed when removal results from a policy violation.

Reporting concerns

Guests should report immediate safety concerns to on-site security or event staff. Additional reporting channels will be published with final event details.
