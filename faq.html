FAQ

Questions before
the reunion?

Here’s what we can share now. More details will be added as tickets, venues and programming are confirmed.

Good to know.

What is Nigerian Reunion?

It is a multi-day cultural weekend built around community, music, food, business, fashion, nightlife and connection—with different experiences throughout the weekend.

When is Nigerian Reunion?

Nigerian Reunion is coming in 2027. Full dates and the official schedule will be announced when they are ready to be released.

I'm not Nigerian. Can I attend?

Yes. We welcome all cousins, friends, and family to our celebration. Nigerian Reunion is about culture, connection, community, and bringing people together.

Where will the events take place?

Multiple venues will be used throughout the weekend. Official locations will be announced as each experience is confirmed.

Will I need one ticket for the whole weekend?

Weekend passes and individual event tickets are being planned so guests can choose how they want to experience the reunion.

Will there be VIP options?

Yes. Premium experiences, VIP sections and reserved tent options are being developed for select events.

Is Nigerian Reunion family-friendly?

Some experiences will welcome families, while certain nightlife or alcohol-focused events may have age restrictions. Each event listing will clearly state its admission policy.

Will there be food and drinks?

Yes. Food and beverage will be a major part of the weekend, with curated menus, bars and hospitality experiences across select events.

Can I become a vendor?

Yes. Vendor and business opportunities will be announced for select events. Join the early list and choose vending or brand opportunities.

Can my company sponsor Nigerian Reunion?

Yes. Sponsorship and brand activation opportunities will be available across the weekend. Join the early list and select sponsorship opportunities.

Will there be opportunities for creators and media?

Yes. Creator, media and content partnership opportunities are being developed. Application and credential information will be released closer to the event.

Can artists, DJs or hosts submit for consideration?

Yes. Artists, DJs, hosts and performers can submit interest through our Talent Interest form. Submission does not guarantee a booking.

Will hotel or travel information be available?

Yes. Travel guidance and recommended hotel information will be shared as lodging partnerships are finalized.

How do I get updates first?

Join the early list for ticket announcements, schedule updates, venue information, VIP releases, vendor opportunities, sponsorship information and reunion news.
