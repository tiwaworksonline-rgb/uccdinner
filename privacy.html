PRIVACY

Your information.
Your choices.

Effective October 1, 2026. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it and how you can manage your information.

1. Information we collect

When you join an interest list, register, purchase a ticket or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, city/state, country, selected interests, investment-interest ranges, travel interests and other information you voluntarily provide.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate Nigerian Reunion, respond to inquiries, send requested updates, segment communications by interest, manage ticketing and event operations, improve our website, prevent fraud and abuse, and support vendor, sponsor, media, travel or investment-related inquiries.

3. Email and marketing communications

If you join an interest list or otherwise request updates, we may send communications related to the categories you selected. You may unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the unsubscribe link in an email. Operational messages related to an active registration or purchase may still be sent when necessary.

4. Service providers

We may use service providers for website hosting, email delivery, contact management, analytics, ticketing, payments, security and event operations. These providers may process information on our behalf under their own privacy and security obligations.

5. Brevo

Our lead and email communications may use Brevo as a contact-management and email service provider. Information submitted through our website may be transmitted securely to Brevo for contact storage, segmentation and communication management.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal information to advertisers. We may share information with service providers, event partners where necessary to fulfill a requested service, or when required by law, safety obligations or legal process.

7. Cookies and analytics

The website may use essential cookies, hosting logs or analytics technologies to understand site performance and usage. If additional advertising or tracking technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

8. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain event and business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce agreements.

9. Your choices

You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate recordkeeping requirements. You may also opt out of marketing communications at any time.

10. Children

This website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Family-oriented event programming, if offered, will have its own admission and supervision requirements.

11. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Policy changes

We may revise this policy as our services evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date.
