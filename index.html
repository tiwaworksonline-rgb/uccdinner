THE DIASPORA EXPERIENCE

More than a weekend.
A diaspora homecoming.

Nigerian Reunion is a global homecoming centered on Nigerian culture, community, entertainment, business and opportunity — while welcoming the wider diaspora and friends of Nigerian culture to participate, connect and experience it with us.

It’s a space to reconnect, celebrate, build relationships, discover opportunities and strengthen connections between the diaspora and Nigeria.
