ABOUT NIGERIAN REUNION

A global homecoming.
Rooted in Nigeria.
Open to the world.

Nigerian Reunion is a global homecoming centered on Nigerian culture, community, entertainment, business and opportunity — while welcoming the wider diaspora and friends of Nigerian culture to participate, connect and experience it with us.

It’s a space to reconnect, celebrate, build relationships, discover opportunities and strengthen connections between the diaspora and Nigeria.

01

Our Mission

Bring people together through Nigerian culture, community and shared experiences that create meaningful relationships, celebrate identity and strengthen connection to Nigeria.

02

Our Goal

Build more than an event — a global platform where culture meets opportunity through entertainment, business, travel, investment, creativity and community.

03

Our Commitment

Create an inclusive, welcoming and high-quality experience that represents Nigeria with pride, opens doors for collaboration and leaves people more connected.
