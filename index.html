FAQ

Questions before
the reunion?

I'm not Nigerian. Can I attend?

Yes. We welcome all cousins, friends, and family to our celebration. Nigerian Reunion Weekend is about culture, connection, community, and bringing people together.

What is Nigerian Reunion Weekend?

It is a multi-day cultural weekend built around community, music, food, business, fashion, nightlife and connection—with different experiences throughout the weekend.

When is Nigerian Reunion Weekend?

Nigerian Reunion Weekend is coming in 2027. Full dates, event times and the official weekend schedule will be announced soon.

Where will the events take place?

Multiple venues will be used throughout the weekend. Official locations will be announced as each venue and experience is confirmed.

Will I need one ticket for the whole weekend?

Weekend passes and individual event tickets are being planned so guests can choose how they want to experience the reunion. Full ticket options will be announced before sales open.

Will there be VIP options?

Yes. Premium experiences, VIP sections and select reserved options are being developed for several events throughout the weekend. Details will be released with ticketing.

Is Nigerian Reunion Weekend family-friendly?

Some weekend experiences will welcome families, while certain nightlife or alcohol-focused events may have age restrictions. Each event listing will clearly state its admission policy.

Will there be food and drinks?

Yes. Food and beverage will be a major part of the weekend, with curated menus, bars and hospitality experiences planned across select events.

Can I become a vendor?

Yes. Vendor and business opportunities will be announced for select events. Join the early list and select vending or brand opportunities so you receive information when applications open.

Can my company sponsor Nigerian Reunion Weekend?

Yes. Sponsorship and brand activation opportunities will be available across the weekend. Join the early list and select sponsorship opportunities to be among the first to receive partnership information.

Will there be opportunities for creators and media?

Yes. Creator, media and content partnership opportunities are being developed. Application and credential information will be released closer to the event.

Will hotel or travel information be available?

Yes. Travel guidance and recommended hotel information will be shared as the weekend gets closer and lodging partnerships are finalized.

How do I get updates first?

Join the early list to receive ticket announcements, schedule updates, venue information, VIP releases, vendor opportunities, sponsorship information and other reunion news.
