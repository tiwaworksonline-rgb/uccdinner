EVENT INFORMATION

Know before
you go.

These are the general operating policies for Nigerian Reunion. Event-specific rules may vary by venue and will be posted with final ticket information.

Accessibility

We aim to make event experiences accessible and will publish venue-specific accessibility information as locations are finalized. Guests who need an accommodation should contact the event team in advance when possible so available options can be coordinated with the venue.

Age requirements

Age policies may differ by event. Daytime or family-oriented programming may differ from nightlife or alcohol-focused experiences. The applicable minimum age will be displayed on each event listing and ticket page.

Entry and re-entry

Entry, re-entry, wristband, credential and security-screening rules will vary by event and venue. Guests must follow posted entry procedures and retain any wristband, ticket or credential required for re-entry.

Food and beverages

Outside food and beverages may be restricted at certain events. Alcohol may not be brought into licensed event areas unless explicitly permitted. Food allergies and dietary needs should be discussed directly with the relevant food operator when possible.

Bags and prohibited items

Bag-size limits and prohibited-item lists will be published for each venue where applicable. Commonly restricted items may include weapons, illegal substances, fireworks, drones, glass containers and items that create a safety or operational risk.

Weather

Some programming may occur outdoors. Events may continue in ordinary weather conditions. Severe weather may cause delays, temporary suspension, relocation, schedule changes or cancellation based on venue and safety guidance.

Artists and schedule changes

Artists, DJs, speakers, hosts, set times and programming are subject to change. A lineup change does not automatically entitle a guest to a refund unless otherwise stated in the ticket-specific purchase terms.

VIP sections and tents

VIP sections, tables, cabanas and tents may have capacity limits, check-in requirements, service minimums, age restrictions and separate policies. Final inclusions will be listed before purchase.

Photo and video

Professional cameras, recording equipment and commercial shoots may require credentials. Personal photography is generally subject to venue rules and the privacy and safety of other attendees.

Lost property

Lost-and-found procedures will be announced for each event. We are not responsible for lost, stolen or unattended personal property.

Travel and accommodations

Unless specifically sold as part of an official package, attendees are responsible for their own transportation, lodging, visas, passports, insurance and other travel arrangements.

Final rules

Venue-specific rules control when they are more restrictive than these general policies. Final operational details will be published as venues and ticketing are confirmed.
