CIVIC ENGAGEMENT + GOVERNANCE

Know the system.
Use your voice.

A nonpartisan space for civic education, public service, governance conversations and community participation—designed to help people understand how decisions are made and how to engage constructively.

PROGRAMMING AREAS

Civic knowledge should be practical.

01

How Government Works

Clear conversations about local, state and federal roles, public agencies, budgets, boards and how policy moves from idea to implementation.

02

Voter Education

Nonpartisan information about registration, election processes, ballot research and how to evaluate public information before making your own voting decisions.

03

Public Service + Leadership

Paths into boards, commissions, public-sector careers, neighborhood leadership and community service.

04

Policy + Community Issues

Issue-focused discussions on business, housing, education, transportation, public safety, immigration, economic development and other community priorities.

05

Diaspora + Governance

Conversations about how diaspora communities engage institutions, build civic networks and contribute expertise across borders.

06

Civic Organizations

Space for nonpartisan civic groups, public agencies and community organizations to share resources, programs and ways to participate.

WHAT THIS IS NOT

Informing people—not telling them how to vote.

This portal is designed for civic literacy and participation. Nigerian Reunion Weekend does not use this programming to endorse candidates, political parties or ballot choices.
